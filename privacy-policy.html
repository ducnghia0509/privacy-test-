Privacy Policy — ducnghia0509

Last updated: August 18, 2026

"ducnghia0509" is an internal tool used solely by our marketing team to automatically publish video content to our company's own TikTok account. This app is not distributed publicly and is not used by any external users.

Data We Access via TikTok's API

  • Basic profile information (display name) of the TikTok account that authorized this app, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access to the Content Posting API (video.publish scope), used only to publish video posts submitted by our internal team to the connected TikTok account.

Data We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect or process data belonging to any TikTok users other than the account owner who explicitly authorized this app.
  • We do not sell, share, or use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than publishing the account owner's own content.
  • We do not use TikTok data to train machine learning models.

Data Storage & Retention

Access tokens and refresh tokens obtained from TikTok are stored securely on our private server and are never shared with third parties. Video content submitted for posting is stored temporarily and deleted after publishing is complete.

Data Deletion

The account owner may revoke this app's access at any time from their TikTok account settings. Doing so immediately invalidates our stored tokens; we delete any remaining stored credentials for that account upon revocation.
